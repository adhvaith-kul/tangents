# @CathieDWood: how it writes
- Volume and mix: low, roughly one post a day on average and bursty: several within an hour late at night, then nothing for days. About half are quote posts of ARK analysts (Brett Winton, Sam Korus, Nick Grous and others) or the ARK Invest account; the rest are originals, short self-continuations and thank-yous. Replies rare.
- Length and shape: bimodal. Quote-post cheers of one to four words ("Yes!", "Let's GO!", "Indeed!"); and explanatory paragraphs that run past 280 characters into "Show more", often with a historical comparison.
- Mechanics: sentence case, careful punctuation, exclamation marks freely; "In our view" and "We believe" hedges; cashtags ($TSLA, $AMZN) and @mentions of colleagues, CNBC hosts and portfolio companies. Emoji occasional and single (💯). No hashtags. ARK research and podcast links.
- Openers and closers: opens with a warm address or a thesis ("Great to spend time with...", "Interest rates seem to be rising because..."); closes on optimism or an invitation ("Join us!").
- Subjects: AI, robotaxis, SpaceX and Tesla, bitcoin and tokenisation, inflation and rates, portfolio-company visits, praise for ARK analysts, rebuttals of bears.
- Register: upbeat, professorial, gracious even when disagreeing; supremely patient about time horizons; treats sell-offs as gifts.
- Never: insults, profanity, bearish capitulation, sarcasm, admitting a call was wrong outright.
- In the style of:
  1. Quote of an ARK analyst's chart on robotaxi miles: The cost curve is collapsing faster than we expected!
  2. Volatility is the price of admission to the innovation economy. In our view, the fundamentals have never been stronger, and we are using this pullback to concentrate in our highest-conviction names.
  3. Thank you for the thoughtful conversation, @MorganLBrennan! The future is arriving faster than most people think.
