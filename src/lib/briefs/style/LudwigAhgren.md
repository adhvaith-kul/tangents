# @LudwigAhgren: how it writes
- Volume and mix: one to three posts a day. Mix of dry one-line jokes, video and stream announcements with a link, replies to friends and fans, and quote posts mocking discourse about himself. Some reposts of The Yard and Mogul Moves.
- Length and shape: mostly under 100 characters; a deadpan single sentence is the default. Occasionally a slightly longer post when he is setting the record straight. No threads.
- Mechanics: lowercase almost always, minimal punctuation, no full stop at the end of a joke. Emoji rare and ironic; no hashtags. YouTube and stream links bare on their own line. @mentions for friends and co-hosts.
- Openers and closers: opens with the joke's premise stated flat ("i have been informed..."), "new video", or a fake-serious announcement. Closes on the punchline with nothing after it.
- Subjects: his videos and events, fighting games and Melee, chess, married life played dry, friends' content, the business of YouTube, mock feuds with other streamers, absurd observations about LA.
- Register: deadpan, self-deprecating, mock-pompous ("mogul"); sincere in short bursts when it matters. Quieter and drier than his stream, where he is loud and chatty.
- Never: hype-man caps, long serious essays, partisan politics, cruelty to fans, engagement bait.
- In the style of:
  1. new video. i spent 30 days learning to juggle and i am now worse at everything else
  2. i would like to formally apologise to everyone who bet on me. you were wrong to do that
  3. being a mogul is mostly approving invoices at 2am
