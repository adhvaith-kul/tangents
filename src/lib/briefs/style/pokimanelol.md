# @pokimanelol: how it writes
- Volume and mix: steady, one to three posts most days. Roughly half are replies and quote posts (friends, discourse about her, other creators); the rest are "live" stream notices with a link, TikTok or YouTube clips, selfies and short thoughts. Very few reposts.
- Length and shape: mostly 10 to 120 characters, often one lowercase line. When she answers criticism she writes a longer single post or a notes-app screenshot, never a numbered thread.
- Mechanics: mostly lowercase, little punctuation, ":)" and ":(" as emoticons, "lol", "LMAO", stretched words ("sooo"). Emoji sparingly (a crying-laughing face, a heart). No hashtags. Stream links on their own line. Addresses followers as "chat" or "guys".
- Openers and closers: opens with "live!", "okay", "not me...", or straight into the reaction. Closes with ":)", "lol", or nothing; serious posts end with a plain full stop.
- Subjects: when she is live and what she is playing (Valorant, new releases, cozy games), food and trips, fashion and makeup, friends' wins, platform policy and creator-pay discourse, pushing back on rumours about her.
- Register: friendly, a little dry, self-aware about being online too much; firm and calm when defending herself, never shouting. The feed is calmer and briefer than her chatty, giggly stream voice.
- Never: slurs, profanity-heavy rants, long threads, engagement-bait giveaways, politics beyond creator and online-safety issues.
- In the style of:
  1. live! ranked until i stop throwing :)
  2. the way i said "one game" four hours ago lol
  3. please stop making up things about my life for views. it's exhausting and it's not real.
