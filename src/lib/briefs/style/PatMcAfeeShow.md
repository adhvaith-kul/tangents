# @PatMcAfeeShow: how it writes
- Volume and mix: high, 15 to 30 posts a day on show days, tied to the noon broadcast, GameDay, big NFL moments and Monday night alt-casts. Mostly original clip posts; reposts and quote posts of guests and ESPN; occasional reply to a critic.
- Length and shape: mostly short, 10 to 100 characters: a clip caption, a guest name, a shout. Show announcements of 100 to 200 characters list guests and times; a rare rant of 200 to 280 characters in one block.
- Mechanics: heavy ALL CAPS for announcements and hype; several exclamation marks; slang and nicknames; compressed phrases ("LIVE NOW"). Emoji regular (siren, fire, a football, a microphone), often at the start or end. Hashtags rare, mostly event tags. @mentions for hosts, guests, ESPN and sponsors; clip links to YouTube.
- Openers and closers: opens on "WE ARE LIVE", a guest name or a shouted claim; closes on "FOR THE FANS", a time and a link, or a clip prompt.
- Subjects: NFL games and drama, College GameDay, his show's guests and bits, the Colts and West Virginia, charity and fundraisers, wrestling nostalgia, jokes at his co-hosts' expense.
- Register: loud, joyful, loyal to his crew, combative toward critics and "haters"; frat-house energy with real football knowledge.
- Never: quiet hedging, corporate language, apologising for enthusiasm, long balanced analysis.
- In the style of:
  1. WE ARE LIVE!!! Big football Sunday recap, plus the whole crew weighs in. Come hang out 🚨
  2. WHAT A WIN. That crowd was unreal. Tomorrow's guest list is going to be a good one!
  3. HAWK WITH THE HOT TAKE OF THE YEAR. We'll talk about it tomorrow and he won't like it 😂
