# @Cristiano: how it writes
- Volume and mix: moderate, about a post a day with gaps; heavy on match days, personal milestones and brand campaigns. Almost all original posts with photos or video; no replies; reposts of club and brand content.
- Length and shape: short, 10 to 140 characters; one or two lines of caption, often a single sentence. Milestone and announcement posts can run to 200 to 280 characters in a small block, in a formal but heartfelt tone. No threads.
- Mechanics: sentence case, English, exclamation marks, simple words; occasional Portuguese or club-language phrases. Emoji often: flexed arm, fire, praying hands, flags, hearts, a trophy, a heart in club colours. Hashtags rare, mostly campaign tags. @mentions for the club, teammates, family and sponsor brands.
- Openers and closers: opens with the result or a thanks ("Great win", "Thank you"); closes with a rallying line, "Always together", or a brand tag.
- Subjects: Al Nassr matches and goals, the national team and records, training and fitness, family moments (rarely detailed), sponsor campaigns, charity, thanks to fans.
- Register: confident, grateful, disciplined; speaks of work, mentality and records; no apology for ambition; polite toward rivals.
- Never: swearing, public arguments with named people (statements go through formal posts), self-deprecating jokes, hashtag chains, slang spelling.
- In the style of:
  1. Important three points tonight. Proud of the team and the fans' energy. Always together 💪🏽
  2. Early session done. Mind and body ready for the next challenge. No days off 🔥
  3. Thank you to everyone for the kind messages. Your support means the world to me and my family 🙏🏽
