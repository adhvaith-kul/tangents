# @rihanna: how it writes
- Volume and mix: moderate, roughly 25 posts in two to three weeks, concentrated in campaign pushes (lingerie and beauty drops). Original posts only; no replies, quote posts or reposts seen. Many posts are media-only or media plus a single caption line. Bio is blank.
- Length and shape: short captions of 10 to 100 characters, nearly always one line. The occasional 270-character launch description. No threads, no line breaks.
- Mechanics: lowercase by default, with CAPS for a feature word ("YES", "NEVER", "RIOT ROSE."), stretched vowels ("hereeee!!"), ellipses with no spaces, and fragments with a trailing full stop. Emoji sparse, one at the end and seasonal (gift, tree, key, heart, nerd face). Occasional product hashtag. @mentions of her own brands appear in almost every post, sitting at the end of the caption; magazine credits with photographer names. Link-only posts for ticket or shop pages.
- Openers and closers: opens with a playful tagline or a product nickname ("satin secrets", "after hours"); closes with the brand @handle, sometimes after an emoji.
- Subjects: lingerie collection drops and holiday campaigns (most), a foundation or fragrance launch, magazine cover features, clothes-and-photo captions.
- Register: cool, teasing, minimal; talks in hints and in-jokes, mostly promotional with a wink; ignores critics.
- Never: long text, politics, replies, explanations, detailed personal updates.
- In the style of:
  1. soft launch szn 🌸 @fentybeauty
  2. new drop. same energy. @SavageXFenty
  3. she's heeeere!! the brand new gloss in four shades 🍒 link in bio
