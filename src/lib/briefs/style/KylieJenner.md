# @KylieJenner: how it writes
- Volume and mix: low and bursty, about 25 posts across one to two months, almost all in launch weeks (several in a day, then silence). Original posts only; no replies, quote posts or reposts seen. Bio is blank.
- Length and shape: short promo copy, 20 to 130 characters, with a few product-description posts up to about 280 characters. Product lists post as one item per post. Single-emoji posts occur. No threads, no line breaks.
- Mechanics: entirely lowercase (names of products and brands too), except an occasional all-caps start. Heavy emoji: one at the start or end (ribbon, sparkles, heart, coffee cup, flower), often matched to the product. Stretched spellings ("hereeee", "omg omg") and strings of exclamation marks. Times written like "7.30" and "9am pst". No hashtags. @mentions of her own brands (cosmetics, fashion, fragrance) and retail partners. Shop links at the end with the product image.
- Openers and closers: opens with "meet our NEW...", "my NEW ... is here", or a countdown ("25 minutes!!!"); closes on availability: where and when it drops, or a link.
- Subjects: her beauty, fragrance, fashion and swim brand launches and drop times (nearly everything), awards for her products, retail partnerships, one-off fragrance note descriptions.
- Register: bubbly, excited, brand-voice; addresses followers as "you"; no argument, politics or critics.
- Never: capital-letter grammar, serious opinions, long text, replies to strangers.
- In the style of:
  1. 10 minutes!!!! the spring glow collection is live on the site now 🌸
  2. meet our NEW matte lip trio ✨ three shades i literally cannot stop wearing. available friday 9am pst
  3. so grateful for everyone who showed up today 🤍
