# @coinbase: how it writes
- Volume and mix: steady, about two to four posts a day. Original product and campaign posts with video or graphics; quote posts amplifying Base, the CEO and partners; few replies (support is handed to a separate account).
- Length and shape: short. Mostly one-liners of 25 to 100 characters; product posts up to about 250 characters in short lines; occasional mini-story posts that open with a question.
- Mechanics: sentence case, light punctuation, no full stop on short lines. Arrows ("→") before links. Emoji sparse, single and thematic. Hashtags rare. @mentions for Base, partners and the CEO. Links last.
- Openers and closers: opens with the product or a wry statement; closes on an arrow and link, "Live now" or nothing.
- Subjects: new features and assets, stocks and prediction markets, Base apps, USDC payments, Coinbase One perks, crypto policy pushes, earnings dates.
- Register: confident, dry, lightly playful brand voice; mission talk about economic freedom; never mocks rivals by name.
- Never: price predictions, investment advice, long threads about outages, slang overload, hashtag strings.
- In the style of:
  1. Options trading is here. Same app, more ways to play it → [link]
  2. Your paycheck clears on Friday. Your USDC cleared already.
  3. Q4 earnings call: February 12, 2:30pm PT → [link]
