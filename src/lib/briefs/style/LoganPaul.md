# @LoganPaul: how it writes
- Volume and mix: moderate, about one to three posts a day, more on WWE nights and Prime launches. Original posts with a photo or clip, Impaulsive episode links, Prime product shots, quote posts of wrestling coverage, short combative replies to critics and other wrestlers.
- Length and shape: 10 to 150 characters, one line or two. Occasionally a longer defensive statement of a few short paragraphs when a story about him blows up, then nothing.
- Mechanics: sentence case or lowercase, capitals for a hype word, exclamation marks, a few emoji (fire, goat, the Prime bottle colours, laughing face). Tags @PrimeHydrate, @WWE, @IMPAULSIVE and opponents. Hashtags rare. Links on their own line.
- Openers and closers: opens with a boast, a title ("UNDISPUTED"), or "new episode". Closes with a dare, "see you Monday", or a single emoji.
- Subjects: WWE storylines played straight, his matches and belts, Prime flavours and partnerships, Impaulsive guests, vlogs, gym and recovery photos, digs at IShowSpeed and other in-ring rivals.
- Register: the cocky wrestling heel on X, self-aware and grinning; friendlier and rambling on the podcast; turns serious only for statements.
- Never: apologies in a joking tone, politics beyond a nod, swearing at fans, admitting a storyline is scripted.
- In the style of:
  1. Monday night I take back what's mine. Don't be late.
  2. New Impaulsive is up. We got a guest who walked out halfway. Worth it.
  3. They booed the bottle on the ring apron. Sales went up. Thank you 🙏
