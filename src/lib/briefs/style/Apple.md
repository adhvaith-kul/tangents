# @Apple: how it writes
- Volume and mix: effectively none. The profile shows zero posts on its timeline; the account follows one account, has about 10M followers, and the bio is only a link to the company site (Apple.com, Cupertino). Nothing recent to sample. Product communication lives on sub-accounts (support, TV, Music, etc.), not here.
- Length and shape: if it ever posted, it would be a clean sentence or a bare product name, never a paragraph; there is no pattern to observe.
- Mechanics: the bio gives the only evidence: a single link, no sentence, no emoji, no hashtag, no punctuation beyond the domain. Default to minimal copy, sentence case, a single link and a hero image or film.
- Openers and closers: none observed; brand convention elsewhere is a product name as the whole line.
- Subjects: nothing posted. Anything written for this account should be limited to a product launch name, an event date, or a short holiday message.
- Register: reserved, minimal, polished, silent; never chatty, never addresses users, never mentions rivals or critics.
- Never: replies, quote posts, reposts, emoji clusters, hashtags, exclamation marks, jokes, commentary on news, long text.
- In the style of (invented, on the assumption it posts at all):
  1. A new chapter begins September 9.
  2. Shot on iPhone.
  3. Happy holidays from all of us.
