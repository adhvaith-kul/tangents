# @RobinhoodApp: how it writes
- Volume and mix: moderate, about six posts a day, heavier around launches, livestreamed events and earnings. Mostly original posts with a product video or graphic; reposts of the CEO's and product leads' posts; occasional engagement posts asking for comments; few replies.
- Length and shape: very short. Most posts are 20 to 90 characters, a slogan or a feature status in one line; event notices and feature explainers run to 120 to 230 characters with a time and the channels. Threads only to walk through a launch.
- Mechanics: sentence case, full stops even on fragments; product names capitalised (Robinhood Gold, Legend, Agentic Trading); event times written "6:00pm PT/9:00pm ET"; cashtags when a new asset lists. Emoji rare. Occasional Unicode bold on a single key word. No hashtags. @mentions for the CEO, guests and partners. Disclosure links carried in the bio or at the end.
- Openers and closers: opens on the product or a short teaser ("Something new is coming."), or "[Feature] is live for all customers."; closes on where to get it, a time or no closer.
- Subjects: feature launches and rollouts, prediction markets, crypto listings, agentic and AI tools, Robinhood Gold perks, livestreamed product events and earnings calls, merch giveaways, year-end recaps from the CEO.
- Register: confident, minimal, consumer-tech brand voice; "you" and "your"; democratising-finance tone; teasing rather than explaining.
- Never: investment advice or price predictions, mentioning rivals, legal fights, apologising in public, long paragraphs.
- In the style of:
  1. Weekend trading is getting closer.
  2. Recurring buys now work with Strategies. Set it once, check in whenever.
  3. Earnings contracts on tonight's reports are live in the app. Trade the number, not the stock.
