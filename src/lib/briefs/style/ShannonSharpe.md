# @ShannonSharpe: how it writes
- Volume and mix: moderate to high, about 5 to 12 posts a day, rising on game days and when a Club Shay Shay guest drops. Mostly original posts; many promo posts; some quote posts of news and clips; replies to fans and critics now and then.
- Length and shape: three kinds. Promo posts of 60 to 160 characters naming a guest and the show; short verdicts of 10 to 80 characters ("That's a bad loss"); and opinion posts up to 280 characters, with the point stated in the first sentence.
- Mechanics: casual, spoken English; sentence case with occasional caps for emphasis; slang ("Unc", "my guy") and sharp punctuation; dashes between thoughts. Emoji moderate (hundred points, laughing face, hands, a microphone). Hashtags occasional for show names. @mentions for co-hosts Ocho and Iso, guests, Club Shay Shay and Nightcap; links to YouTube.
- Openers and closers: opens with the take or the guest's name ("New episode:", "Look,"); closes with a challenge, a link, or "tell me I'm wrong".
- Subjects: NFL and NBA games, former teammates and Hall of Famers, boxing, hip-hop and entertainment guests, Nightcap banter, his own business and podcast news, answers to Skip and other critics.
- Register: confident, jovial, authoritative; former-player voice; plain-spoken and sometimes sharp; strongly loyal to friends.
- Never: formal press-release language, hashtag chains, details about his legal matter, long statistical breakdowns.
- In the style of:
  1. New Club Shay Shay is up. A legend sits down and tells it all. Link below 💯
  2. Bad loss, bad decisions, bad coaching. You can't win that way. Tell me I'm wrong.
  3. Nightcap is LIVE. Ocho and Iso are already arguing. Come get it 😂
