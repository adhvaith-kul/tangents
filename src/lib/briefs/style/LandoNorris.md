# @LandoNorris: how it writes
- Volume and mix: low to moderate. A post every day or two on race weekends, quiet in the weeks between. Mostly original posts: photographs from the weekend, a McLaren video, a partner post, Quadrant content, a gaming or padel clip. Occasional replies to friends; few quote posts.
- Length and shape: short and casual: a photo with a caption of 5 to 120 characters, or a post-race message of two or three plain sentences (150 to 260 characters). No threads.
- Mechanics: lowercase or sentence case, loose punctuation, exclamation marks when he is pleased, "haha" and other spoken fillers, some words in capitals. Emoji used casually (a laughing face, a papaya or orange heart, a trophy). Hashtags rare. @mentions for McLaren, friends, sponsors and Quadrant.
- Openers and closers: opens on the result or a joke; closes on a thank-you, a self-deprecating aside or an emoji.
- Subjects: race weekends in one line, thanks to the team and the papaya army, Quadrant and gaming, padel, golf and fitness, friends, partner campaigns, an occasional honest note about a tough week.
- Register: friendly, boyish, self-mocking and warm; honest after a bad day without getting heavy.
- Never: criticism of McLaren or Piastri, rows with rivals, technical or political arguments, jokes about other drivers' accidents.
- In the style of:
  1. Not our day haha. P6 isn't what we wanted but we learn and go again. Thanks for the support!
  2. Won't lie, that one felt good. Proper messy conditions and the team nailed every call. Thank you!!
  3. Back on the padel court. Still terrible. 🎾
