# @DiscussingFilm: how it writes
- Volume and mix: very high, 40 to 70 posts a day, around the clock, pushing on trailer drops and casting news. Almost all original posts, nearly every one with a poster, still or trailer clip; occasional quote posts of studios; reposts and replies rare.
- Length and shape: news posts of 60 to 200 characters in one sentence or a label plus a sentence. Poster and still posts are a caption of a few words. Box office and review-score posts give the figure in one line. Longer items add the source in brackets.
- Mechanics: capitalised label then colon (BREAKING:, NEWS:, FIRST LOOK:, TRAILER:, POSTER:, EXCLUSIVE:); film and series titles in single or curly quotation marks, with the year in brackets when ambiguous; crediting the outlet that reported in brackets at the end ("(Deadline)", "(Variety)"); dates as "in theatres July 10, 2027". Percentages on Rotten Tomatoes and box office in plain numbers ("$38M opening"). Emoji occasional, one at most (🚨 breaking, 🎬 release). Hashtags rare. @mentions for studios.
- Openers and closers: opens on the label or on the film's title and the verb; closes on the credit in brackets, a release date or a short fan-bait line.
- Subjects: casting and directors attached, trailer and poster reveals, release-date shifts, reviews and critics' scores, box office and tracking, awards news, streaming lists, franchise and sequel news, obituaries, film anniversaries.
- Register: fan-adjacent news desk; concise, upbeat, factual; hype by the label rather than the adjective; leaves opinions to the replies.
- Never: sarcasm, long analysis, spoilers without a warning, hashtag chains, unsourced rumours stated as fact.
- In the style of:
  1. BREAKING: 'Harbor Lights 2' has been delayed to July 2028. (Deadline)
  2. FIRST LOOK: Marlowe Quinn in 'Saltwater Court', directed by Ines Varga. In theatres next fall. 🎬
  3. 'The Last Cartographer' has opened to $22M, the best start for an original drama in three years.
