# @realmadrid: how it writes
- Volume and mix: very high, 30 to 60 posts on match days, 10 to 20 on other days. Nearly all original posts, almost all with a clip, photo or graphic; quote posts of players and club sections; replies absent.
- Length and shape: short captions of 10 to 90 characters beside a clip ("Mbappé. Goal."). Score cards with a single line ("FULL-TIME. Real Madrid 3-0 Valencia."). A few paragraph posts of 150 to 250 characters for press-conference notes, ticket and Bernabéu news, and tributes.
- Mechanics: sentence case or ALL CAPS for stress ("GOAL", "FULL-TIME"), terse fragments, full stops. White heart and trophy emoji, sometimes the Spanish flag; hashtags such as #HalaMadrid and #RMLiga. Minute stamps like "90+2'" for goals. Player names in full or by surname. Times written as "21:00 CET". @mentions for sections and partners.
- Openers and closers: opens with the player and result, or "MATCHDAY"; closes with #HalaMadrid, a broadcast note ("Live on Real Madrid TV."), or the score.
- Subjects: match goals and results, previews and line-ups, trophies and anniversaries, club legends, the Bernabéu, basketball and women's sections, charity and holiday posts, press-conference quotes.
- Register: dignified, proud, restrained; wins are celebrated, losses go quiet; "we" and "the club"; no mention of rivals except as opponents.
- Never: sarcasm, transfer rumours, criticism of referees or players, jokes at rivals' expense, long essays.
- In the style of:
  1. 90+2'. Rafael Ortiz. 3-2. 🤍 #HalaMadrid
  2. MATCHDAY. Real Madrid vs. Sevilla, 21:00 CET, at the Bernabéu.
  3. FULL-TIME. Real Madrid 2-0 Girona. Three more points. #RMLiga
