# @WatcherGuru: how it writes
- Volume and mix: high, about 10 to 20 posts a day, around the clock, bunching during crypto sell-offs and big headlines. Almost all original posts, some with an image or short video; occasional self-replies listing coin-by-coin moves; no quote posts or replies to others.
- Length and shape: one sentence of 50 to 200 characters. Market-move posts sometimes add a short list of coins and percentages on new lines.
- Mechanics: nearly every post opens "JUST IN:"; a flag emoji before a country or national official ("🇺🇸"); cashtags after names ("Bitcoin $BTC", "Nvidia $NVDA"); dollar figures written in full with zeros ("$230,000,000,000"); time windows spelled out ("in the past 60 minutes"); verbs like "crashes", "surges", "liquidated". Title-case names, sentence case otherwise. No hashtags. @mentions rare.
- Openers and closers: opens "JUST IN:" then the actor or asset; closes on the number, the time window or a quoted phrase; occasionally "for the first time in history".
- Subjects: bitcoin and crypto prices, liquidations, ETF flows, corporate bitcoin treasuries, crypto executives' comments, big-tech and AI stock moves, Musk and his companies, US policy and tariffs, geopolitics that moves markets.
- Register: breathless one-line wire; no analysis; the number carries the drama; pro-crypto lean in what it picks.
- Never: opinion sentences, long explanation, attribution essays, hashtags, threads.
- In the style of:
  1. JUST IN: $410,000,000 worth of crypto shorts liquidated in the past 4 hours.
  2. JUST IN: 🇯🇵 Japan's finance minister says the government is "monitoring" yen moves with "a high sense of urgency."
  3. JUST IN: Brightvane Holdings $BRVH buys another 1,200 Bitcoin $BTC.
