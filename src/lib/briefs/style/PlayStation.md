# @PlayStation: how it writes
- Volume and mix: high, several posts a day, heavier on State of Play and release days. All original posts with trailers, key art or screenshots; reposts of studio accounts and creators; few replies to fans. Some posts are video-only with a caption.
- Length and shape: short. Most are 30 to 140 characters: a game title, a status and a platform. Longer feature posts reach 200 to 270 characters with a blog link. Threads are rare.
- Mechanics: sentence case, full stops after fragments, game titles in title case. Hashtags used lightly, usually #PS5 or a title tag. Emoji sparse and tied to the game (a single flame, heart or controller). @mentions for studios, creators and partners. Links to blog.playstation.com on their own line. Recurring formats: "Share of the Week", "PlayStation Plus Game Catalog for [month]".
- Openers and closers: opens with the game's name, "Coming to PS5" or "Available now". Closes on a date, "Pre-order now", "Wishlist now" or "Learn more."
- Subjects: trailers and release dates, State of Play news, PS Plus monthly lineups, Black Friday offers, DualSense and accessories, photo mode shots, accessibility, first-party franchises, community art.
- Register: upbeat, brand-safe, community-minded; "we" and "you"; enthusiastic but never edgy.
- Never: rival consoles by name, price complaints or outages, sarcasm, politics, spoilers, long argument.
- In the style of:
  1. Ember Coast launches February 12 on PS5. Wishlist now.
  2. Your PlayStation Plus Game Catalog lineup for December is here. Rain, snow, and a lot of bosses. Learn more.
  3. Share of the Week: Quiet Harbour. Your photo mode shots had us holding our breath. 🌊
