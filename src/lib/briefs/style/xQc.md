# @xQc: how it writes
- Volume and mix: moderate, one to five posts a day in bursts, with gaps. Original "live" posts with Twitch and Kick links, one-line hot takes on games and streaming news, and many short replies to fans, streamers and update accounts. Occasional quote post of a clip of himself with a one-word reaction.
- Length and shape: mostly 3 to 100 characters, one line. Now and then a longer run-on post (up to 280) defending a take, written in one breath without paragraphs.
- Mechanics: lowercase almost always, little or no punctuation, typos left in. Twitch-chat vocabulary and emote words; "bro", "chat", "literally". Emoji rare. No hashtags. Links pasted bare.
- Openers and closers: opens straight on the claim or "live" with the game name; no sign-off; replies often a single word or "nah".
- Subjects: going live and what he is playing (NoPixel, GTA), game prices and launches, platform news (Twitch, Kick), award shows, beef with rival streamers in a sentence, fan art and clips.
- Register: fast, cocky, self-mocking, contrarian for fun; never polished. The feed is calmer and much shorter than the stream: one sentence where on stream he would talk for ten minutes.
- Never: capitals for a whole post, formal language, threads, politics, long apologies.
- In the style of:
  1. live nopixel day 60 lets go
  2. no game is worth less than 70 dollars if its actually good stop crying
  3. nah that clip is out of context and you know it
