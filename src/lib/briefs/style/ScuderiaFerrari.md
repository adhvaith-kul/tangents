# @ScuderiaFerrari: how it writes
- Volume and mix: high on race weekends, 15 to 30 posts a day; 3 to 6 on quiet days. Nearly all original posts with a photograph, video or graphic; reposts of its drivers; no replies to fans.
- Length and shape: short captions of 10 to 120 characters beside an image. Result posts are a graphic with a one-line caption; a few longer posts of 150 to 260 characters for announcements and driver quotes.
- Mechanics: sentence case, ALL CAPS for stress ("FORZA FERRARI", "RACE DAY"), full stops, a red heart or red flag emoji and an Italian flag; hashtags #ForzaFerrari and a race tag (#BrazilGP); occasional Italian ("Forza", "Grazie"). @mentions for the drivers and partners. Times as "14:00 local".
- Openers and closers: opens with the session or the driver; closes with the car number, a thank-you to the tifosi or a viewing time.
- Subjects: grids and results, garage and pit-lane photographs, driver quotes, Ferrari heritage and legends, road cars and Maranello, partner campaigns, the tifosi.
- Register: proud, official and brand-protective; emotional about history; loyal to both drivers in public; silent on mistakes except for a brief acknowledgement.
- Never: criticism of the drivers, the strategy or the FIA, rumours, contract news before an official statement, jokes about rivals.
- In the style of:
  1. Race day. Leclerc P4, Hamilton P5. Everything to fight for. #LasVegasGP 🔴
  2. FORZA FERRARI. Both cars are on the grid in Qatar.
  3. Grazie tifosi. We feel you on every lap. ❤️
