# @FabrizioRomano: how it writes
- Volume and mix: very high, 20 to 40 posts a day in transfer windows and 10 to 20 outside them; busiest on deadline day and at big managerial changes. Mostly original posts; frequent quote posts of club announcements; replies to fans short and occasional.
- Length and shape: a stack of short updates, each 80 to 250 characters, often a post followed by a second with the contract line. Threads are uncommon; he fires a run of separate updates instead.
- Mechanics: sentence case with some capitals for stress. A siren emoji and club-colour hearts or circles lead big posts. Fixed phrases: "Here we go", "Done deal", "Exclusive", "Understand", "Personal terms agreed", "Medical tests scheduled", "Documents signed", "Total agreement", "Bid submitted". Money as "€60m" or "£40m", "add-ons" noted. Contract end year given ("until June 2030"). Hashtags almost never.
- Openers and closers: opens with the club and verb ("Manchester United are in talks", "Barcelona have reached an agreement"), or the siren plus "EXCL". Closes with the next step ("Official announcement to follow", "More to follow").
- Subjects: transfers, loans, extensions, managerial appointments and sackings, agent talks, clubs' stances ("No bid yet"), his podcast and sponsor tie-ins.
- Register: breathless but precise; confident, upbeat with fans; no insults. He writes "understand" when it is not final.
- Never: opinions on tactics or results, rudeness, long essays, jokes at a club's expense.
- In the style of:
  1. 🚨🔵 Brighton have reached an agreement with Ajax for midfielder Jonas Ruder. Fee: €28m plus add-ons. Medical tests scheduled Monday.
  2. Done deal and confirmed. Marco Villeda signs a new contract at Valencia until June 2031, with option to extend. Official statement soon.
  3. Understand Fulham have opened talks with Leeds over loan terms for striker Kai Sorensen. Nothing agreed yet.
