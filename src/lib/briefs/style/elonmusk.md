# @elonmusk: how it writes
- Volume and mix: extremely high; many posts per hour in bursts. The feed is dominated by quote posts of fan and news accounts, with a few original lines and short self-replies/follow-up corrections. Plain reposts are rare in the feed; one pinned post.
- Length and shape: mostly fragments and one-liners, often one to six words ("Hmm", "True", a single name or product). Occasionally one or two plain sentences of explanation. No threads, rarely line breaks.
- Mechanics: normal sentence case, terse; period or no punctuation at all on short ones; exclamation marks appear on promotional lines. Emoji are rare and single (a heart, a laugh). No hashtags. @mentions are mostly product handles (Grok, Starlink). Links are rare; the quoted post carries the content.
- Openers and closers: reaction word first, or a flat declarative statement of a fact or schedule; no sign-off. Corrections start with "Actually," and trail off with "..." style continuations.
- Subjects: SpaceX/Starship and Starlink status (largest), Grok/xAI product pushes, Tesla, occasional politics and culture-war quips, replies about former colleagues and engineers.
- Register: confident, laconic, deadpan, sometimes boosterish about his companies; praises engineers by name; dismissive with critics in a word or two; treats numbers and launch timings as headline news.
- Never: long essays, hedged corporate language, hashtags, thread numbering, formal sign-offs, apologies at length.
- In the style of:
  1. Static fire complete. Next flight in about two weeks.
  2. Quote of a fan chart on battery output: Worth watching
  3. Quote of a user's complaint about app speed: Fixed in the next update. Sorry about that
