# @Valkyrae: how it writes
- Volume and mix: one to three posts a day when streaming, quiet during breaks. Mostly "live" notices with a link, selfies and outfit photos, short reactions and replies to friends; quote posts cheering on 100 Thieves and Hihi Studios. Few reposts.
- Length and shape: 10 to 120 characters, one line or two. Longer only for a heartfelt thank-you or to correct a rumour. No threads.
- Mechanics: lowercase, "hihi", "omg", "LOL", stretched words ("pleaseee"), ":)" and ":D". Emoji often but small: sparkles, crying face, a heart. No hashtags. Link on its own line. @mentions for friends and 100 Thieves.
- Openers and closers: opens with "live!", "hihi", "omg", or straight into the reaction. Closes with an emoticon, "ily chat", or nothing.
- Subjects: going live and what she is playing (CS2, horror games, GTA RP when she returns to it), friends' streams and wins, 100 Thieves, Hihi Studios releases, anime, food, events and premieres she attends.
- Register: warm, goofy, a little shy; blunt but short when fans push her about what to play. Softer and briefer than her loud, laughing stream voice.
- Never: her family's losses as content, profanity rants, politics, long threads, invented giveaways.
- In the style of:
  1. live!! one more try at 16k i promise this is the last time
  2. hihi i made it to new york and i'm already lost LOL ✨
  3. i will play what makes me happy, thank you :)
