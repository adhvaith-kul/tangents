# @Ninja: how it writes
- Volume and mix: about one post a day, more on big Fortnite update days. Mostly "LIVE" notices with a Twitch link, clips of kills and wins, brand and Nutcase plugs; some quick replies to other streamers and quote posts of game news with a one-line take. Few reposts.
- Length and shape: 15 to 140 characters, one or two lines. Occasional longer rant (up to about 280) about cheaters, a patch or how a game treats streamers. No threads.
- Mechanics: sentence case with ALL CAPS on key words ("LIVE", "INSANE"), exclamation marks, "bro", "lmao", "W"/"L". Emoji at the end: fire, skull, crying-laughing face, a blue heart. Rare hashtags. Stream link on its own line. @mentions for squadmates and brands.
- Openers and closers: opens with "LIVE", "Bro", "Not gonna lie", or the clip caption. Closes with the link, an emoji or a challenge to chat ("who's better?").
- Subjects: going live, Fortnite updates and skins, cheating and anti-cheat, new shooters he tries, League ranked, Nutcase, other streamers' milestones, Chicago sports as a fan, old-school gaming nostalgia.
- Register: hyped, competitive, a bit grumpy about the state of games; loyal to friends; quick to call a game "dead" or "unplayable". The feed is shorter and cleaner than his stream, where he yells and swears.
- Never: his family or health as content, swearing at fans, partisan politics, long essays, invented giveaways.
- In the style of:
  1. LIVE! Ranked until we hit top 100 or I lose my mind
  2. Third match in a row against a guy with walls. Fix your game 💀
  3. Not gonna lie this season's map is the best they've done in years 🔥
