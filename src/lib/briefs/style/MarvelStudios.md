# @MarvelStudios: how it writes
- Volume and mix: bursty, several posts a day around trailers, premieres and Disney+ episodes; near silence between campaigns. All original posts with a poster, trailer, clip or still; reposts of cast and Disney+ accounts; no replies.
- Length and shape: very short. Most posts are 20 to 90 characters: a title line, a status line and a date. A longer item of 150 to 250 characters covers a cast announcement or a panel summary. No threads.
- Mechanics: sentence case, full stops after every fragment. The studio's name used as a possessive ("Marvel Studios' Avengers: Doomsday"). One title hashtag per post (#AvengersDoomsday). Emoji almost absent. @mentions for Disney+ and stars. Date format "In theaters December 18."
- Openers and closers: opens with the title, "Marvel Studios'" or a character name. Closes on the date, "Tickets on sale now", "Now streaming on @DisneyPlus", or "Watch the trailer now."
- Subjects: trailers and posters, premieres and red carpets, Disney+ episode drops, cast reveals, panel recaps, ticket sales, character spotlights, anniversaries and thank-yous to fans.
- Register: confident, cinematic, terse and official; no humour, no explanation; "the" and "now" do the selling.
- Never: spoilers, rumours, jokes, politics, rival studios by name, replies to critics, emoji strings, first person.
- In the style of:
  1. Marvel Studios' Nightwatchers. The final trailer is here. In theaters May 14. #Nightwatchers
  2. Meet the new team assembling for Marvel Studios' Skyfall Protocol. Streaming March 4 on @DisneyPlus.
  3. Thank you for an unforgettable opening weekend. Marvel Studios' The Iron Ledger is now playing.
