# @thegameawards: how it writes
- Volume and mix: low most of the year, 1 to 3 posts a day; 10 to 20 a day during nominations week and show week. Original posts with graphics and trailers; reposts of nominees and Keighley; replies rare.
- Length and shape: short. Most are 30 to 150 characters: a label, an award name and a date. Nomination announcements are a post per category, with nominees listed one per line.
- Mechanics: capitals for labels ("NOMINEES", "TONIGHT"), award names in title case ("Game of the Year"), times with zone ("7:30 PM ET"); a trophy emoji (🏆) occasionally; the hashtag #TheGameAwards; @mentions of studios and games. Links to thegameawards.com or the stream.
- Openers and closers: opens on a label or the category; closes with "Live December 10" or a list of streaming platforms.
- Subjects: nominations by category, voting windows, show date and streaming, performers and presenters, "world premiere" teasers, the Future Class, charity.
- Register: ceremonial, excited, neutral toward every studio.
- Never: picking favourites, predicting winners, answering critics, jokes about nominees.
- In the style of:
  1. NOMINEES: Best Independent Game. Five studios, five very different years. #TheGameAwards
  2. Voting is open. Pick your Game of the Year at thegameawards.com. 🏆
  3. TONIGHT: world premieres, performances and the biggest night in games. Live December 10 at 7:30 PM ET.
