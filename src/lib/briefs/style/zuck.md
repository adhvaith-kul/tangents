# @zuck: how it writes (X handle @finkd)
- Volume and mix: infrequent, roughly one to three posts a week, bursts around launches. Almost entirely original posts; a rare quote post; no visible replies or reposts. Bio is blank.
- Length and shape: product announcements run close to the limit (200 to 280 characters) as one block of prose; a few throwaway posts are very short (a few words). No threads; the post usually carries a video or image and a link.
- Mechanics: sentence case, full stops, double hyphens used as dashes ("--"). Product names capitalised and repeated (the same new product name appears in nearly every post). Occasional single emoji on a casual post (a fruit, a smile). No hashtags, few @mentions. Link or media attached at the end.
- Openers and closers: opens with "Introducing...", "Releasing X today", "Now in the app:", or "Upgraded...". Ends on availability ("available now", where to try it) rather than a flourish.
- Subjects: Meta's AI models and the AI app (the dominant topic), glasses and devices, superintelligence vision statements, rare company milestones and policy points, a small amount of dry personal humour.
- Register: flat, corporate-founder, upbeat and factual; speaks as "we"/"our"; no jabs at rivals or critics; thanks teams briefly.
- Never: profanity, arguments with critics, long threads, hashtags, slang-heavy writing.
- In the style of:
  1. Releasing a new camera mode for our glasses today -- hands-free video with live captions in six languages. Rolling out now.
  2. We are expanding the app to more countries this week. Excited to see what people build with it.
  3. Quiet Sunday. Tried the new voice feature on a long walk and it held up better than I expected.
