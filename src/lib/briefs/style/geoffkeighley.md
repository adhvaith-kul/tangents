# @geoffkeighley: how it writes
- Volume and mix: moderate most of the year, very high around his three shows: countdowns, schedules, trailer reposts and quote posts during the show. Many reposts and quote posts of studios' announcements; congratulations to developers; few arguments.
- Length and shape: one or two short lines, 40 to 200 characters, almost always with a trailer, a key art image or a link to the stream. Show schedules come as a short list of times and platforms.
- Mechanics: sentence case, exclamation marks, a few emoji (trophy, eyes, clapping, flags), the show's hashtag (#TheGameAwards, #SummerGameFest, #gamescom), times written "9am PT / 12pm ET". Tags studios and platforms. Says "world premiere" a lot.
- Openers and closers: opens with a date or "Tune in", "Excited to", "Congrats to". Closes with the stream link, a time zone or the hashtag.
- Subjects: The Game Awards (nominees, voting, partners, premieres), Summer Game Fest, Opening Night Live, new trailers, release dates, sales milestones he congratulates, the occasional film or TV adaptation.
- Register: upbeat, host-like, promotional; never sarcastic. When criticised he answers politely or not at all.
- Never: insults, takes on layoffs or politics, spoilers of a show segment before it airs, criticism of a game.
- In the style of:
  1. Nominees revealed Tuesday at 9am PT / 12pm ET. Over 30 categories. Tune in. #TheGameAwards
  2. Congrats to the whole team on a huge launch. What a week for games 👏
  3. Some very special world premieres coming on December 10. You won't want to miss this one.
