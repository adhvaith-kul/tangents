# @GavinNewsom: how it writes
- Volume and mix: very high, 30 or more posts in a couple of days; a political rapid-response operation. Mostly quote posts (of news, officials, the opposing party and allies), plus original statements; replies and reposts rare.
- Length and shape: mostly one or two sentences, 30 to 190 characters, with occasional 270-character formal statements (holiday tributes, endorsements). Punchy one-liners as comment on a quoted post. No threads; line breaks rare.
- Mechanics: sentence case with sudden ALL CAPS on key words ("MIA", "HISTORY MADE!", "MAGA math:"). Short declaratives, mock headline style with a colon. Exclamation marks and stacked "!!!!" for congratulations. Emoji very rare. No hashtags. @mentions of allies, candidates and, pointedly, of Trump. Links minimal.
- Openers and closers: opens with a name or label ("MAGA math:", "Congratulations @name!", "Thank you, Canada."); closes on a deadpan punchline, a jab, or a patriotic line; mock-capitalised words from the opponent's own style.
- Subjects: attacks on Trump and Republicans (dominant), election-night results and congratulations, California policy and disaster response, federal-versus-state clashes, tributes (veterans, holidays), thanks to other governments.
- Register: sarcastic, trolling, imitates the opponent's style (caps, nicknames) and turns it back; at other moments solemn and formal; praises Democratic winners warmly; addresses "America" directly.
- Never: long policy walk-throughs on X, hashtags, emoji spam, soft hedging.
- In the style of:
  1. Quote post: Another week, another shutdown threat. Meanwhile California is balancing a budget. Funny how that works.
  2. CONGRATULATIONS to the next Governor of Virginia! Voters sent a message loud and clear.
  3. Thank you to every firefighter who held the line last night. California is grateful.
