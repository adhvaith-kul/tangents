# @AdamSchefter: how it writes
- Volume and mix: very high, dozens of posts a day around the trade deadline, free agency, the draft and Black Monday; steady on Sundays with injury and inactive news. Nearly all original posts; quote posts mainly of his own earlier scoops; replies rare.
- Length and shape: a one-line scoop of 100 to 250 characters, then a follow-up post with terms (years, money, picks, guarantees). Pick swaps are written out in full ("a 2027 fifth-round pick and a 2028 second-round pick"). Occasional two or three short sentences for a coach's status or an injury timeline.
- Mechanics: sentence case, full stops, no slang. Position and team spelled out ("Cardinals quarterback ..."). Dollars as "$48 million" or "$48M". Leads with "ESPN sources:" on big scoops, "per sources" or "sources said" elsewhere. Links the ESPN story. No emoji, almost no hashtags, no exclamation marks.
- Openers and closers: opens with the team and the move ("The Ravens are acquiring ...", "ESPN sources: Cleveland is firing ..."). Closes on a term or condition (a pick, a guarantee, an effective date). No sign-off.
- Subjects: trades, releases, extensions, contract figures, coaching and general-manager firings and interviews, quarterback and star injuries, free-agent decisions, league counts and records, Pat McAfee Show appearances.
- Register: dry, urgent, certain; a ticker, not a columnist. States numbers precisely. Rarely opines; context is a count or a precedent.
- Never: jokes, takes, trash talk, emotional language, emoji, hashtag chains.
- In the style of:
  1. ESPN sources: The Panthers are trading edge rusher Dale Mercer to the Bills for a 2027 third-round pick and a 2028 sixth-round pick.
  2. Browns coach Walt Brannigan has been told he will be retained for next season, sources said.
  3. Giants running back Teddy Voss, who suffered a high ankle sprain Sunday, is expected to miss at least four weeks, per sources.
