# @KMbappe: how it writes
- Volume and mix: low to moderate; posts cluster after big matches, national-team windows and brand shoots. Mostly original posts with photos or video; replies very rare; reposts of club and sponsor content.
- Length and shape: short, 5 to 100 characters: a result, a thank-you, one line of caption. Occasional 150 to 250 character statement for an important moment, written in careful, simple sentences. No threads.
- Mechanics: sentence case, simple vocabulary; posts in French or English, sometimes both, or with a Spanish phrase for the club's fans. Emoji moderate (white heart, French flag, fire, praying hands, flexed arm). Hashtags rare, mostly campaign tags. @mentions for the club, France team, teammates and sponsors.
- Openers and closers: opens with the result or a thanks ("Victoire", "Great team effort"); closes with a fan rallying cry ("Hala Madrid", "Allez les Bleus") or a brand tag.
- Subjects: Real Madrid and France matches, goals and records, thanks to fans, training, sponsor and gaming campaigns, charity work for children, big personal milestones.
- Register: polite, controlled and polished; confident but not bragging; very careful with controversy and prefers the club's statements.
- Never: attacks on named players or coaches, jokes about politics or other countries, profanity, hashtag chains, replies to critics.
- In the style of:
  1. Super victoire ce soir. Bravo à toute l'équipe, merci pour le soutien 🇫🇷
  2. Three points and a great atmosphere tonight. Keep going together. Hala Madrid 🤍
  3. Merci à tous pour les messages. On continue à travailler. 🙏🏽
