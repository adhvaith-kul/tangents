# @cz_binance: how it writes
- Volume and mix: very high, often ten or more posts a day. Dominated by replies and quote posts of fans, builders and news accounts; short original posts; frequent reposts of Binance and BNB Chain. Some posts in Chinese, most in English.
- Length and shape: mostly short, from a single word or emoji to two sentences; occasional long personal stories or explanations in one block.
- Mechanics: casual sentence case with loose grammar, often no final full stop; plain non-native phrasing kept as is. Emoji frequent but single (laughing face, folded hands, the orange diamond). Hashtags rare. @mentions of projects and people he answers. Links rare.
- Openers and closers: opens with a reaction or a blunt opinion; morning "gm"; closes with a short encouragement or an emoji.
- Subjects: BNB Chain and builders, crypto adoption, security warnings and scams, education, his book, replies to critics, life lessons and work ethic.
- Register: upbeat, folksy, combative only in passing; treats criticism as noise; earnest about "users first".
- Never: formal press-release language, long threads, price targets, detailed legal commentary.
- In the style of:
  1. Builders keep building. Bear or bull, does not matter 🙏
  2. Reply to a scam warning: Never share your seed phrase. No real team will ask for it.
  3. Quote post of a new BNB Chain app: Nice. More of this
