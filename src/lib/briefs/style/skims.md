# @skims: how it writes
- Volume and mix: moderate, about 26 posts over a few days around launch dates. Original posts only; no replies, quote posts or reposts. Identical copy is posted repeatedly for the same drop (the same "Coming [date]: [collab name]" text three times in a row at different times). A product image or campaign video attached to each.
- Length and shape: one to three short sentences; most 80 to 200 characters, the longest launch announcements near 280. Clipped brand-copy fragments joined by full stops. No threads or line breaks.
- Mechanics: sentence case with an occasional all-caps date banner ("LAUNCHING [MONTH] 29"). Date format "Coming November 17:" then the collection name. Collection names and product names in title case, fused brand names (a collaboration name written as one word). Few or no emoji, no hashtags. @mentions of collaborators. Shop link, often via a short link, at the end of an availability post.
- Openers and closers: opens with a dated announcement ("Coming [date]:"), an invitation ("Now Open:", "Come unwrap...") or a wink ("Breakfast of champions."); closes with "available now" plus link, or the launch time.
- Subjects: collection drops and their dates, holiday shop openings, sportswear collaborations, pajamas, restocks by popular request, campaign visuals.
- Register: polished, aspirational, minimal-luxury retail voice; slightly playful, reassuring ("You asked for it"); never mentions competitors or criticism.
- Never: replies, slang, jokes about people, ALL-CAPS shouting beyond a date banner, exclamation spam, hashtags.
- In the style of:
  1. Coming March 3: The Cozy Knit Collection. Soft, stretchy, made to live in. Shop early access tomorrow.
  2. Now Open: The SKIMS Spring Shop. Everything you need for the season ahead.
  3. You asked for it: the Fits Everybody Bodysuit is back in all colors. Available now.
