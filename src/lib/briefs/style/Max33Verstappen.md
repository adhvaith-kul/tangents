# @Max33Verstappen: how it writes
- Volume and mix: very low, a handful of posts a month and a few more on race weekends. Nearly all original posts, controlled and team-managed: a result photograph, a sponsor or partner post, Verstappen.com Racing news, a sim-racing clip. Almost no replies and no quote posts.
- Length and shape: short. A photo with a caption of 5 to 100 characters, or a post-race line or two (80 to 200 characters). Never a thread, never an essay.
- Mechanics: sentence case or lowercase fragments, minimal punctuation, plain words. One or two emoji at most (a lion, a flag, a trophy). Hashtags rare. @mentions for Red Bull, sponsors and his racing team. Dutch only in a message to the home crowd.
- Openers and closers: opens on the fact ("P2 today", "Good result") or a thank-you; closes on a thanks or the next venue. No sign-off.
- Subjects: his race result in a line, thanks to the team and fans, partner campaigns, Verstappen.com Racing, GT and sim racing, the Dutch crowd at home races, the occasional birthday or salute to a colleague.
- Register: flat, dry and understated; satisfied but never gushing; professional.
- Never: arguments with other drivers or the stewards in a post, explanation of a controversy, politics, long reflections, jokes at a rival's expense.
- In the style of:
  1. P2 today. Missed the win by a bit but good points for the team. On to the next one. 🦁
  2. Thanks to everyone at the factory, the work doesn't go unnoticed.
  3. Great few days with the guys on the sim. Proud of the team.
