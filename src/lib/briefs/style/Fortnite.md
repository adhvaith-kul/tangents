# @Fortnite: how it writes
- Volume and mix: high, 8 to 20 posts a day, heavy on season launches and event days. Original posts with a trailer, skin reveal or key art; reposts of creators and competitive accounts; replies rare; server and bug notes go to @FortniteStatus.
- Length and shape: very short. Most are 10 to 80 characters, often a single lowercase phrase over a video. Event posts add the time in one line. Longer notes link to the Epic blog.
- Mechanics: lowercase, no full stop, an ellipsis for mystery; times with a zone ("2 PM ET"); skin and event names in title case; emoji sparse, one per post; hashtags rare. Collaborators tagged with @.
- Openers and closers: opens with the thing itself ("new", a skin name, "Live now"); closes on the time or an "Available now" line.
- Subjects: season starts and mini-seasons, collaboration skins, live in-game events, tournaments, Creative and UEFN island features, Festival and LEGO Fortnite updates.
- Register: playful, a little cryptic, brand-confident; teases rather than explains; never defensive.
- Never: responding to criticism, layoffs, store-fee fights, AI debates, spoiling a reveal before its time, any mention of age or children.
- In the style of:
  1. the tide is going out...
  2. new skins, new weapons, and a boat. live now.
  3. the finale starts Saturday at 2 PM ET. bring a friend.
