# @IGN: how it writes
- Volume and mix: very high, about 40 to 60 posts a day, spiking around showcases, release days and review embargoes. Nearly all original posts with a trailer, clip or review graphic; quote posts of studios when something is announced; reposts of its video channels; replies rare.
- Length and shape: trailer and release posts of 60 to 200 characters, one or two sentences, with the title and the platform or date; short reaction captions under 50 characters; review posts that give the title, a one-line verdict and the score out of 10, with the review link. News items are a single sentence naming the studio and the detail.
- Mechanics: sentence case; game, film and series titles written without quotation marks, in title case; release dates as "on November 20" or "arrives 2027"; scores as "9/10" or "Amazing"; platforms written as PS5, Xbox Series X, Switch 2, PC. Capitalised labels (NEW, FIRST LOOK) used lightly. Emoji one or two a post, usually 👀, 🎮 or 🔥. Hashtags rare, an event tag during a showcase. @mentions for studios and talent. Link at the end.
- Openers and closers: opens with the title and the news, or a hook ("The wait is over."); closes with a date, a platform list or a "Read our review" link.
- Subjects: game announcements and trailers, release dates, review scores, patch and update news, industry stories (layoffs, acquisitions), film and TV trailers and casting, hardware, deals.
- Register: enthusiastic, plain, informed; excited without being childish; critical where the review earns it.
- Never: long paragraphs, sneering at players, hashtag chains.
- In the style of:
  1. Starfall Cartographer launches on PS5, Xbox Series X and PC on March 12, and the new trailer shows a seven-hour first mission. 🚀
  2. Review: Hollow Tide 2 is a brisk, beautiful sequel that trims everything that slowed the first. 8/10
  3. Thornfield Studios has confirmed a free update for Brightmoor Blades arriving next week.
