# @KingJames: how it writes
- Volume and mix: low to moderate, in bursts around games, big family moments and business launches. Mostly original posts, many with a photo or video; occasional reposts of friends and teammates; replies uncommon.
- Length and shape: mostly short, 10 to 120 characters: a reaction, a salute or a one-line caption. Now and then a longer, earnest paragraph of 200 to 280 characters about family, faith or a cause, written in one block.
- Mechanics: casual, loose capitalisation; some words in caps for emphasis ("WOW", "LET'S GO"); repeated exclamation marks; short fragments. Emoji used freely and in pairs or strings (crown, praying hands, flexed arm, salute, heart). Hashtags occasional (a team or project tag). @mentions for teammates, friends, his media companies and brands.
- Openers and closers: opens straight in with the reaction ("Man...", "Wow", "Congrats to") or a name; closes on a blessing, a catchphrase or an emoji string rather than a link.
- Subjects: his own team and teammates, his kids' games and milestones, friends' wins, his business and media projects, sports and music moments that caught him, thanks to fans and hometown.
- Register: warm, proud, loyal; the elder statesman cheering others on; occasionally pointed about disrespect or fairness, never crude.
- Never: long policy essays, public trash talk of a named opponent, jokes about his kids, formal corporate language.
- In the style of:
  1. Big night from the young fella. Proud of that group! Keep working 🙏🏾💪🏾
  2. Two weeks of pure joy at the lake with the family. Grateful for every minute. Back to work!! 👑
  3. Congrats to the whole crew on the new spot. Come hungry, leave happy. LET'S GO 🔥
