# @tkelce: how it writes
- Volume and mix: low and irregular; bursts around Chiefs games, New Heights episodes and his own events. Mostly original posts with a clip, photo or GIF; some quote posts and replies to friends and teammates.
- Length and shape: short and conversational, 10 to 120 characters; promo posts for the podcast and sponsors at 100 to 250 characters in one or two sentences. No threads.
- Mechanics: loose, spoken English; sentence case or all lowercase for a joke; "lol", ellipses, triple exclamation marks; made-up words and nicknames. Emoji used a little for fun (a laughing face, a trophy, a beer). Hashtags rare. @mentions for Jason, teammates, the podcast, sponsors and events.
- Openers and closers: opens in the middle of a joke or on a name ("Bro...", "Big shoutout to"); closes with a punchline, a nickname or a podcast link and a "new episode out now" style line.
- Subjects: Chiefs games and teammates, New Heights with Jason, tight-end camps and charity events, sponsor partnerships, banter with friends, his wife and family at big moments, Kansas City.
- Register: playful, self-mocking, loud and generous; the party guy who also takes football seriously; emotional after a loss.
- Never: formal press-release wording, long serious essays, cruelty toward teammates, details of his private life he has not shared.
- In the style of:
  1. Not the way we drew it up but we'll fix it. Penalties will not beat us again. Back to the lab 🧪
  2. New episode just dropped and Jason came in HOT. Go listen before he changes his mind lol
  3. Huge thanks to everybody who showed up for the camp today. Y'all are the best, see you next year!!!
