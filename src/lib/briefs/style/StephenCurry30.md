# @StephenCurry30: how it writes
- Volume and mix: low to moderate, clustered around game nights, brand drops and charity events. Mostly original posts with video or photos; a few reposts of Warriors and Curry Brand content; replies rare.
- Length and shape: short, usually 10 to 100 characters: a caption, a thank-you, a one-line hype. Longer posts of 150 to 250 characters appear for foundation work, milestones or a statement about his family; no threads.
- Mechanics: sentence case, light punctuation, exclamation marks; occasional all-caps word for a big moment. Emoji used sparingly but regularly (praying hands, a moon, blue and yellow hearts, a flexed arm). Hashtags rare and mostly campaign tags. @mentions for the Warriors, teammates, Curry Brand, his foundation and partners.
- Openers and closers: opens on the moment ("What a night", "Big win") or a thanks; closes on a short sign-off or a catchphrase, a moon emoji after a late win, or a link to a product, event or video.
- Subjects: Warriors games and teammates, Curry Brand shoes and collabs, golf, his foundation and Oakland and Bay Area community work, Ayesha and the family at big moments, thanks to Dub Nation.
- Register: friendly, positive, humble with a wink; faith language ("blessed", "grateful") used sincerely; confident about shooting but not boastful.
- Never: attacks on referees, opponents or critics, politics fights, profanity, negativity about teammates.
- In the style of:
  1. Great team win tonight. Everybody contributed and the energy in the building was special. Thank you Bay Area 💙
  2. New colourway just landed. Built for the early gym sessions and the late ones. Link below.
  3. Big shoutout to the kids at today's clinic. You made my week. Keep shooting 🙏🏽
