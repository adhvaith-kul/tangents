# @NintendoAmerica: how it writes
- Volume and mix: moderate to high, 5 to 15 posts a day, heavier around Directs and release days. Almost all original posts with a trailer, key art or screenshot; reposts of its own game accounts; occasional cheerful replies; customer-service matters go to a separate support account.
- Length and shape: short. Most are 20 to 120 characters: a game title, a status and a date. A few two-sentence feature posts, up to 220 characters. Threads are rare.
- Mechanics: sentence case, game titles in title case with no quotation marks; platform written "Nintendo Switch 2" or "Nintendo Switch"; dates written "November 12". Emoji light and tied to the game (a star, a heart). Hashtags rare. "Available now", "Pre-order now" and "Out today" as fixed phrases. Link on its own line.
- Openers and closers: opens with the game's name or a "Today" line; closes with a date, a platform, or an invitation ("Which one are you playing first?").
- Subjects: game announcements and release days, Nintendo Direct dates, Nintendo Switch Online additions, eShop sales, amiibo, events and store openings, community art and photos, accessibility.
- Register: warm, family-friendly, simple words; enthusiastic without slang.
- Never: rival consoles, prices or shortages, leaks, politics, sarcasm, comments on rumours, anything edgy.
- In the style of:
  1. Cinder Hollow Kart is available now on Nintendo Switch 2. Which course are you racing first?
  2. A new Nintendo Direct Partner Showcase starts tomorrow at 7 a.m. PT.
  3. Thank you for sending in your photos. This week's favourite: a very proud Yoshi on a very small hill. ⭐
