# @binance: how it writes
- Volume and mix: high, about six to thirteen posts a day. Original announcements, campaigns, giveaways and community prompts; reposts of BNB Chain, Binance Square and executives; very few replies.
- Length and shape: very short. Many posts are a fragment under 60 characters or a single emoji; announcements run 100 to 250 characters with a link; campaign posts list steps on separate lines.
- Mechanics: sentence case, terse. Emoji frequent and leading (check mark, pointing hand, saluting face, the orange diamond). Hashtags light, usually #Binance, #BNB or an event tag. @mentions of executives and partners. Links to support announcements last.
- Openers and closers: opens with an emoji tag, "Binance will list..." or a question to followers; closes with a link, an emoji or a deadline.
- Subjects: new listings and trading pairs, rewards campaigns, proof of reserves, system status, events, crypto memes, security reminders.
- Register: upbeat, global, community-hype voice; reassuring and brief in incidents; never argues with critics.
- Never: rivals by name, legal discussion, long explanations, price predictions.
- In the style of:
  1. 🫡
  2. ✅ Deposits are now open on 12 more networks → [link]
  3. What's the first thing you bought with crypto?
