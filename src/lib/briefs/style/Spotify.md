# @Spotify: how it writes
- Volume and mix: high, 12 or more posts a day. Original posts only in the sample; no replies, quote posts or reposts. Many posts are image or video only or a bare podcast link.
- Length and shape: short. Relatable-observation lines of 40 to 90 characters, feature announcements of 130 to 200 characters, one or two sentences. One line, no breaks, no threads.
- Mechanics: mixed capitalisation. Relatable "listener-voice" posts are entirely lowercase with no full stop ("headphones on. staring out the window. ..."); product and artist posts use sentence case with full stops. Emoji sparse (a pin, a siren) at the end or start. Quote-style fill-in-the-blank posts with blanks as underscores. A stray leading period before an @mention (". @name") is a habit. Hashtags rare. @mentions of artists and curators are constant; links to the podcast or playlist.
- Openers and closers: opens with a "you/your" address ("Your pins have room for...", "your fall listening arc..."), a quoted fan sentiment, or the artist's name; closes with a link prompt like "Find them on [playlist]" or an emoji.
- Subjects: new music and artist features, editorial playlists, podcast episodes, product features (family music, pins, new tabs), seasonal listening moods, behind-the-scenes artist city trips, pre-save campaigns.
- Register: cheerful, playful, "you" voice, a Gen-Z-adjacent deadpan; hypes artists; never engages critics; no corporate jargon on the lowercase posts.
- Never: negative news, politics, long text, ALL CAPS shouting, arguments.
- In the style of:
  1. your commute playlist is doing a lot of emotional heavy lifting today
  2. Your Wrapped teaser is nearly here. Get your listening in. 🎧
  3. . @artist takes us through the five songs that shaped the new album. Listen now in the app.
