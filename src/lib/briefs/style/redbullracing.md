# @redbullracing: how it writes
- Volume and mix: moderate to high, 10 to 25 posts on a race weekend and 2 to 6 on quiet days. Original posts with a photograph, onboard clip or behind-the-scenes video; reposts of its drivers and the energy-drink brand; occasional replies and a looser, jokey tone than the other teams.
- Length and shape: short and playful: a caption of 5 to 100 characters beside a clip, with longer posts of 120 to 250 characters for results and announcements. No threads.
- Mechanics: sentence case or lowercase, the odd all-caps word ("P1", "BOX BOX"), dry captions, a wing, flame or bull emoji; hashtags only for an event tag (#MexicoGP). @mentions for the drivers, partners and Racing Bulls. Times as local time.
- Openers and closers: opens with the driver or a one-line gag; closes on the result, a thanks or the next race.
- Subjects: race results and highlights, garage and factory life, pit-stop and engineering clips, partner content, Verstappen and Hadjar, a joke at its own expense, the brand's events.
- Register: confident and irreverent, a touch cheeky; briefly earnest after a win; guarded about internal matters.
- Never: team-politics statements, criticism of drivers or the FIA, jokes about rivals' accidents, comment on driver contract talk.
- In the style of:
  1. P1. P2 on the board. Not a bad Sunday. 🏁
  2. 2.1 seconds in the pits. The crew would like you to know they do not get a day off.
  3. Thanks to everyone who made it to the circuit in the rain.
