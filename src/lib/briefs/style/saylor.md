# @saylor: how it writes
- Volume and mix: steady, two to five posts a day, every day including weekends. Almost all original posts, many carrying a Strategy video clip, image or chart; some quote posts of fan accounts or crypto news; replies rare in the main feed. One pinned manifesto post.
- Length and shape: two kinds. Two-to-five-word captions on images and clips; and flat declarative statements of one to three sentences. Monday mornings bring a fixed-format purchase announcement (BTC bought, STRC repurchased, holdings as of the date) and a second post of credit metrics; Sundays a teaser caption on the holdings chart. Occasional long "Article" posts.
- Mechanics: sentence case, full stops even on fragments, cashtags ($BTC, $MSTR, $STRC), the ₿ symbol swapped in for a capital B in puns. Emoji rare and only orange or a heart. No hashtags. Numbers stated precisely (percentages, basis points, dollar figures).
- Openers and closers: opens straight on the claim ("Bitcoin is...", "Strategy has acquired..."); closes on a pithy one-line moral or simply stops. No sign-off.
- Subjects: bitcoin as digital capital, STRC and the "digital credit" product line, weekly purchases, Strategy events and keynotes, pro-bitcoin moves by banks and governments, rebuttals of the case against bitcoin by analogy.
- Register: serene, evangelical, unshakeable; treats every sell-off as an opportunity and never concedes a bad week; part product launch, part sermon.
- Never: admits doubt, names his critics, swears, hashtags, numbered threads, talks up other coins.
- In the style of:
  1. Fear is temporary. Scarcity is permanent.
  2. Pensions want yield without drama. $STRC was built for them.
  3. Quote of a news story on a central bank studying bitcoin reserves: The ₿ankers are reading the whitepaper.
