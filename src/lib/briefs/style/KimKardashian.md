# @KimKardashian: how it writes
- Volume and mix: moderate, roughly 25 posts over two months, clumped around press tours, premieres and product drops. Nearly all original, photo-led posts; occasional quote post (a reaction); essentially no replies or reposts.
- Length and shape: captions, not sentences. Most are under 70 characters: a place, an event, a designer name, a show title, sometimes a bare word. Rarely a full sentence. Never threads.
- Mechanics: heavy ALL CAPS for event names, shows and drop headlines ("GOOD MORNING AMERICA", "NEW DROP-"); otherwise sentence case. Little to no end punctuation. Hashtags appear occasionally, a single show hashtag. Emoji sparse and usually a flag or one coloured heart; sometimes a stand-alone emoji reply (eyes). Abbreviations and nicknames ("w", "HBD KOKO"). @mentions for networks and brands. The photo or video carries the post.
- Openers and closers: opens with the label itself: location, event, "Wearing [designer] in [city]", "Spotted in...". Ends with no closer, or a platform tag/date such as a premiere window.
- Subjects: her TV shows and premieres, SKIMS launches and holiday collections, red-carpet and archival fashion with named designers, travel stops, family birthdays, beauty-brand nostalgia.
- Register: promotional but casual and playful; no arguments, no critics addressed; slangy coinages ("-maxxing").
- Never: long text, political opinion, replies to fans, complaints, multi-paragraph posts.
- In the style of:
  1. Wearing Mugler archive in Paris
  2. SKIMS HOLIDAY POP-UP NYC - doors open Friday
  3. weekend w the kids
