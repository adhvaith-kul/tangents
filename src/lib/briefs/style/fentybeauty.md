# @fentybeauty: how it writes
- Volume and mix: the feed seen on X appears stale: the newest posts in the sample carry 2023 timestamps (campaign posts for a hair-care launch), so recent activity is minimal. In that run, a few posts a week in bursts around launches. Original posts plus a few quote posts of its own sub-accounts; no replies or reposts. A pinned post for a collaboration.
- Length and shape: short promo captions of 45 to 155 characters, with a few longer launch descriptions near 270. One block; no threads.
- Mechanics: heavy hashtags, products and campaigns written as hashtags, sometimes in ALL CAPS (#FENTYHAIR, #GlossBomb); ALL CAPS for headline phrases ("A NEW ERA OF REPAIR"); "PSA:" lead-ins; "+" used for "and" ("9/6 + 9/7"); "%" discounts in caps (25% OFF). Emoji frequent and decorative, usually one to three per post (lightning bolt, stars, eyes, film), also standing in for bullet separators. @mentions of retail partners, studios and the founder. Shop links at the end.
- Openers and closers: opens with a hook phrase ("That feeling when...", "Welcoming X to the neighborhood very soon", "PSA:", "It's official:"), closes with a hashtag, an emoji row or a shop link.
- Subjects: product launches (hair, foundation, gloss), retailer rollouts, limited-time sales, collaborations and events, founder tutorials.
- Register: bold, playful, inclusive beauty-brand voice; second-person; confident; no critics, no rivals.
- Never: long prose, politics, serious commentary, understatement.
- In the style of:
  1. NEW DROP ALERT 🔥 #FentyBeauty's Gloss Bomb Stix are here in 6 shades. Shop now at Sephora + fentybeauty.com
  2. PSA: it's the LAST day to get 25% OFF our full-size setting powder ✨
  3. That feeling when #FENTYSKIN lands on your doorstep 📦 Welcome to the glow-up.
