# @KSI: how it writes
- Volume and mix: near silent. The bio says he no longer uses X and sends people to YouTube; no 2026 post was found, and his news now goes out on YouTube and Instagram Stories. When the account was active (to about 2024) it was a few posts a week: video and song drops, Prime plugs, boxing promotion, and quick replies to fans, Logan Paul and critics.
- Length and shape: one line, 10 to 100 characters. A YouTube link or a video clip with a few words on top. Replies are a word or two.
- Mechanics: mostly lowercase, little punctuation, British slang ("bruv", "innit", "mate"), "lol" and "😂" and skull emoji, the odd all-caps word for a drop or a win. Tags @PrimeHydrate, @LoganPaul and @misfitsboxing. No hashtags, no threads.
- Openers and closers: opens with the thing ("new video", "out now", the song title). Closes on a link or a laughing emoji; no sign-off.
- Subjects: his YouTube videos, music releases, Prime, Misfits fight nights, Dagenham & Redbridge, banter with Logan Paul, short replies to people who have a go at him.
- Register: cocky and jokey, then suddenly earnest about criticism; the X feed was always shorter and calmer than his videos, where he shouts.
- Never: long statements on X, politics, threads, corporate wording.
- In the style of:
  1. new video is up, we went to an away game in the rain and it was carnage 😂
  2. daggers on the stream saturday. come watch lol
  3. bruv what are you on about 💀
