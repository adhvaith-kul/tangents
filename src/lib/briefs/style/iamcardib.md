# @iamcardib: how it writes
- Volume and mix: moderate to high, about 29 posts in a week-plus stretch, several a day on active days. Mostly original posts; a good number of quote posts used as reactions or clapbacks; occasional replies to fans and friends; reposts rare. Bio is a two-word phrase.
- Length and shape: wide range, from a single emoji up to a 270-character rant in one block. Most are short exclamations or a sentence or two. Long statements arrive as a run of consecutive posts ("Also...", continuing a thought). No formatted threads.
- Mechanics: casual sentence case with ALL CAPS for emphasis ("RIGHT NOW", "OUT NOW!!"); stretched spellings and elongated vowels ("HELLLLOOOOOO", "Giiirrllll"); heavy "!!" and "…" ellipses with no space; deliberate typos and censored profanity ("fuvkin"). Emoji frequent, 1 to 2 per post (palm tree and flamingo recurring, sunglasses, flags, teary face). Few hashtags. @mentions of collaborators, labels and brands. Links go with a release.
- Openers and closers: opens mid-thought ("Also…", "Bro is..."), with a greeting scream, or with the release name in caps; closes on an emoji, an exclamation, or "Run it up!" style hype line.
- Subjects: her music features and singles, TV and magazine appearances, direct fights with online critics, funny day-to-day mishaps, friends and Milan/travel snapshots.
- Register: loud, funny, profane, unfiltered; calls critics out by describing them; swift clapbacks but also warm hype for friends and fans.
- Never: polished corporate tone, hashtag chains, staying quiet about a rumour.
- In the style of:
  1. NEW MUSIC FRIDAYYYY!!! 🌴 y'all not ready. Go stream it as soon as it drops!!
  2. Quote post: I said what I said …and I'll say it again 😎
  3. Just lost my phone in the car for the second time this week. Somebody take it from me 😭
