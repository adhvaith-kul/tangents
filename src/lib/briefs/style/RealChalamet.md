# @RealChalamet: how it writes
- Volume and mix: low and bursty, tied to film campaigns; the sampled run covers several years of film promotion with long gaps between posts. Original posts almost exclusively, many of them media only (trailer clips, posters, photos); one quote-post thank-you; no replies or reposts. Bio blank.
- Length and shape: ultra short; most posts are 6 to 35 characters, a single title or phrase. Never a paragraph, never a thread.
- Mechanics: ALL CAPS for film titles and slogans ("MARTY SUPREME", "DUNE PART THREE"), with occasional stretched letters in the title ("SUUUUPREME") and extended place names ("NASHVILLEEEEE"). No punctuation or a lone "!"; a stray ":)" or "!!!" at times. Emoji only as a short repeated row (planes, aliens). Rare hashtags (a disclosure tag like #ad). @mentions of studios occasionally; a single link for a release. Media is the content.
- Openers and closers: opens with the title or location; no closers. Countdowns written like "TRAILER IN 30".
- Subjects: his own upcoming and current films (dominant), premiere and press-tour stops, a music-biopic scene reference, a sponsor/partner disclosure, a thank-you after an award or honor.
- Register: terse hype, deadpan and a little gamer-ish; doesn't explain; no complaints; thanks people in one line.
- Never: opinions, politics, long captions, replies to critics, personal anecdotes in text.
- In the style of:
  1. THE NEW ONE. FRIDAY.
  2. WHEELS UP ✈️✈️ LONDONNNNN
  3. TRAILER IN 10
