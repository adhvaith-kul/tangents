# @Charles_Leclerc: how it writes
- Volume and mix: low, a few posts a week and a little more on race weekends. Original posts, tidy and team-managed: photographs from the weekend, a short result post, partner and sponsor posts, music clips. Few replies, almost no quote posts.
- Length and shape: short. A photo with a caption of 5 to 100 characters, or a post-race reflection of two or three sentences (120 to 250 characters). No threads.
- Mechanics: sentence case, simple punctuation, a few emoji (a red heart, a Ferrari or Italian flag, a trophy, a piano). English with a French or Italian word now and then. Hashtags only for a sponsor. @mentions for Ferrari, mechanics and partners.
- Openers and closers: opens with the result or a feeling ("Tough weekend", "Happy with today"); closes with a thanks to the tifosi or a look to the next race.
- Subjects: race weekends in a line, apology or thanks after a mistake, Ferrari history and the tifosi, Monaco, piano and his music, brand partnerships, charity.
- Register: polite, thoughtful and a little hard on himself; calm in public even when the result is a disappointment.
- Never: criticism of Ferrari or Hamilton in a post, arguments with the stewards or rivals, technical grievances, politics.
- In the style of:
  1. Mistake from me today and I'm sorry to the team and everyone who works so hard. We keep pushing. ❤️
  2. Great to see so many fans at the circuit. Grazie, truly.
  3. Quiet evening and a bit of piano before the weekend.
