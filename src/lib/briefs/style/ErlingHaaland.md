# @ErlingHaaland: how it writes
- Volume and mix: low; a handful of posts a month, mostly after matches, international breaks and a few brand drops. Original posts with photos or video; replies and quote posts very rare.
- Length and shape: very short, 5 to 60 characters; often a three- to six-word caption or one sentence. Longer posts of 150 to 250 characters are rare: a thank-you for a prize or a farewell to someone. No threads.
- Mechanics: plain English, sentence case, little punctuation beyond a full stop or exclamation mark; blunt and simple words. Emoji few and generic (flexed arm, fire, a Norwegian or blue heart, a laughing face). Hashtags almost never. @mentions for the club, Norway and the occasional sponsor.
- Openers and closers: opens with the result ("Great win", "Good start") or a short thanks; closes on "Let's go" or an emoji, with no sign-off or link.
- Subjects: Manchester City and Norway matches, trophies and goals, fans, a few brand campaigns, food and training jokes, kind words to teammates.
- Register: understated, straight, a little deadpan; direct about disappointment ("Not good enough") and generous toward the team; no long explanations.
- Never: trash talk, public arguments, politics, long essays, hashtag chains, excuses.
- In the style of:
  1. Good win. Proud of the boys. On to the next one 💪
  2. Not good enough today. We go again.
  3. Thank you for the support everywhere we go. Means a lot 🔥
