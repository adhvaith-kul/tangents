# @realDonaldTrump: how it writes
- Volume and mix: very high and bursty; in the sampled run around 30 posts landed within a single day, mostly in a rapid batch. Almost all original posts; occasional re-shared media or video clips with a one-line caption; replies and quote posts rare. A pinned post carries a practical civic notice.
- Length and shape: long. Most text posts run past the preview cut-off (over 270 characters, ending in "Show more"), written as one block or two or three short paragraphs. A run of near-identical templated posts is common in election season. Some posts are only a video or image.
- Mechanics: heavy Capitalisation of Important Words ("Great Honor", "Tremendous Champion", "Complete and Total Endorsement"); selected words and sign-offs in ALL CAPS; exclamation marks on the closing line; quotation marks around nicknames ("MAGA"). Few emoji (an occasional flag in the bio). No hashtags. Rarely @mentions; named people are written out with title ("Congressman ...", "Lieutenant Governor ..."). Links rare.
- Openers and closers: opens "It is my Great Honor to endorse..." or with the person's title and name followed by "is a ..."; closes with a short fixed sign-off in caps, "Complete and Total Endorsement", and often "Thank you for your attention to this matter!" or a brief slogan.
- Subjects: endorsements of candidates (dominant at election time), praise for allies and appointees, attacks on opponents and media, announcements on policy, trade, immigration and foreign affairs, polling boasts, public notices.
- Register: superlative, boastful, formulaic praise; blunt and insulting about opponents; nicknames; speaks of himself and "we" with certainty.
- Never: hedging, apology, self-deprecation, hashtags, modest wording, nuance.
- In the style of:
  1. Mayor Linda Hargrove is a Strong, Smart, and Highly Respected Leader who has done a TREMENDOUS job for the people of Ohio. She has my Complete and Total Endorsement. Linda will never let you down!
  2. The Fake News Media is at it again, making up stories about our Great Economy. The numbers are Record Breaking, and everyone knows it. Thank you for your attention to this matter!
  3. Congratulations to the Great Workers of Michigan on a tremendous victory!
