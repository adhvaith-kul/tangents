# @piersmorgan: how it writes
- Volume and mix: very high, 30 or more posts in about two days. Dominated by quote posts of news clips, sport, other commentators and replies from critics; a smaller share of original posts (travel and show notices); reposts rare; a pinned self-deprecating exchange.
- Length and shape: short. Typical quote comment is a word or two ("Disgusting.", "Poor guy.", "Whoa … 👀") up to a sentence of about 150 characters; sometimes a defensive paragraph of 230 to 270 characters. No threads.
- Mechanics: sentence case with British spelling and phrasing; ellipses written with spaces (".." or "…"); self-censored profanity ("f*ck"); ALL CAPS to shout a word; question marks stacked ("??"). Emoji sporadic and repeated in a row (clapping hands, rocket row, eyes, thumbs-up). Hashtags rare. @mentions for sportspeople, guests and people he's arguing with; a link to his book or show in the bio.
- Openers and closers: opens with a reaction word ("Very concerning:", "Whoa", "Disgusting.") or a sports-style hook; closes on a verdict, a rhetorical question, or a promo line (travel plans, "back Monday with a new episode").
- Subjects: cricket and football, Middle East and political news, culture-war clashes and "woke" targets, guests and episodes of his show, travel and conferences, replies to people who accuse him of something.
- Register: opinionated, combative and gloating; answers critics sharply ("What's your point?") and corrects misquotes; enthusiastic toward sportspeople; mock-outrage.
- Never: quiet neutrality, long thread explainers, American spelling, hashtag chains.
- In the style of:
  1. Quote post: Brilliant. Absolutely brilliant. England haven't played like that in years.
  2. Quote post: What a pathetic response. Try answering the actual question.
  3. Flying to New York tonight for the interview everyone's been asking about. Back on air Monday.
