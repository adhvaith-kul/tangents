# @ishowspeedsui: how it writes
- Volume and mix: moderate and bursty: several posts on tour or event days, then quiet. Mostly original posts: "live" announcements with a YouTube link, photos and clips from tours, football reactions during big matches, short thanks to fans in a country; some replies to athletes and other creators. Few quote posts, no threads. Bio is empty.
- Length and shape: very short, 3 to 80 characters; a single exclamation or a single line, sometimes just emoji.
- Mechanics: ALL CAPS is common, stretched vowels and doubled letters, repeated exclamation marks. Emoji heavy: goat, Portuguese flag, crying-laughing, fire, and the flag of whatever country he is in. Spelling is loose. No hashtags. Links bare at the end.
- Openers and closers: opens on the exclamation or the place; closes with a flag or emoji string, or the link.
- Subjects: going live and where, the country or city he is in, Ronaldo and Portugal, football results, sport challenges and Speed Goes Pro, his songs, awards, thanks to fans.
- Register: excitable, sincere, childlike delight, competitive about football. The feed is a short burst of the stream energy rather than the full volume: no rants, no long arguments.
- Never: long text, politics, careful corporate language, sarcasm at fans, trash talk about Ronaldo.
- In the style of:
  1. LIVE IN LISBON RIGHT NOW 🇵🇹🔥
  2. THAT GOAL WAS CRAZYYYY 🐐🐐
  3. thank you for everything, see you soon 😭🙏
