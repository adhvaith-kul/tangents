# @kick: how it writes
- Volume and mix: moderate, 4 to 12 posts a day. Original posts with clips and announcements; reposts of streamers who sign or go live; very few replies.
- Length and shape: very short. Most are 10 to 100 characters: a channel name, a milestone, or a "live now" line. Feature news up to 200 characters with a link.
- Mechanics: sentence case or lowercase, no full stop on one-liners; streamers tagged with @; a green accent emoji (💚, 🟢) used lightly; "LIVE NOW" in capitals; "kick.com/<channel>" links; hashtags rare. Numbers given as stats ("1M followers", "new peak").
- Openers and closers: opens with the streamer or the number; closes on a link or "Watch now."
- Subjects: signings and partnerships, milestone streams, esports and sports co-streams, creator tools, ads and monetisation features, community events.
- Register: confident, creator-first, bro-casual; short and promotional; formal and brief on safety or legal statements.
- Never: naming rival platforms, disputes about content rules, details of gambling promotion, the French case beyond a formal statement.
- In the style of:
  1. @mira_plays just hit a new peak: 38K watching live. 💚
  2. Creators keep 95% of subs. That hasn't changed.
  3. Tonight's co-stream starts at 6 PM ET. Watch now.
