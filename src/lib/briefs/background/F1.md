# @F1: background (as of 14 November 2026)
- What it is and who leads it: the official account of Formula 1, run by Formula One Management under Liberty Media; chief executive Stefano Domenicali. The 2026 season is the first under the new regulations, with 11 teams (Audi and Cadillac are new).
- Recent:
  - 1 Nov, Mexico City: Kimi Antonelli (Mercedes) clinched the drivers' title at 20 years and 68 days, the youngest champion in the sport's history; Max Verstappen won the race and Lewis Hamilton was second.
  - 8 Nov, Sao Paulo: Lando Norris won in changing weather from Verstappen and Isack Hadjar; Mercedes clinched the constructors' title.
  - Standings: Antonelli about 390 points, Verstappen 279, Russell 262, Hamilton 261, Leclerc 249, Norris 246 (sources differ slightly).
  - Still to run: Las Vegas (21 Nov), Qatar (29 Nov) and Abu Dhabi (6 Dec), the finale.
  - Silly-season and 2027 items: Racing Bulls confirmed Lawson and Lindblad; Red Bull and Racing Bulls named Tsolov as reserve for 2027.
- Products and priorities: the new cars (active aero in place of DRS, 50/50-style electrical power, sustainable fuel), the Apple TV US broadcast deal, F1 TV, Drive to Survive, F1 Academy.
- Publicly at odds or allied with: all ten teams and the FIA; broadcast partners; Netflix, Apple and Liberty Media; criticism of the 2026 rules from some drivers is acknowledged but not argued.
- Handle with care: the new regulations' overtaking debate, driver injuries, safety incidents, team-orders rows and penalties; do not invent race results, penalties or rules rulings as fact.
