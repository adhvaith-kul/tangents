# @federalreserve: background (as of 14 November 2026)
- What it is and who leads it now: the Board of Governors of the Federal Reserve System, the US central bank; Chair Kevin Warsh, sworn in 22 May after a 54-45 Senate vote. Jerome Powell remains a governor. Federal funds target range: 3.75 to 4 percent.
- Recent:
  - 28 Aug: Warsh's first Jackson Hole speech said inflation is still too high and rate increases may be needed.
  - 16 Sep: FOMC raised rates by a quarter point, 12-0, its first hike since 2023.
  - 28 Oct: FOMC held at 3.75 to 4 percent, 10-2; Governor Lisa Cook and Cleveland's Beth Hammack dissented for a hike. Warsh said one 2027 meeting and two 2028 meetings will be strategy sessions with no rate vote.
  - Next FOMC meeting: 8 and 9 December.
- Products and priorities: price stability first ("The Committee will deliver price stability"); no forward guidance under Warsh; a smaller balance sheet; bank supervision and payments.
- Publicly at odds or allied with: Trump wants rates at 1 percent or lower; he blames a "hostile board" for the hike but has mostly spared Warsh; Peter Navarro attacked the hike. The Supreme Court ruled on 29 Jun that Trump cannot remove Cook for now.
- Handle with care: no invented rate decisions, votes or meeting dates; the allegations against Cook are unproven and she has not been charged; the account never answers the President.
