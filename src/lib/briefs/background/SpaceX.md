# @SpaceX: background (as of 14 November 2026)
- What it is and who leads it now: rockets, Starlink, Dragon, and since Feb 2026 parent of xAI/Grok/X. Public since 12 June. Elon Musk is CEO; Gwynne Shotwell is a senior executive (title unconfirmed).
- Recent:
  - 12 Jun: IPO at about $1.77T, the largest ever.
  - 14 Aug: closed the $60B all-stock Cursor purchase.
  - 13 Nov: Starship Flight 15 reached orbit and deployed 26 Starlink V3 satellites; the booster crashed in the Gulf, no recovery.
- Products and priorities: Starship, Starlink, lunar work for NASA, AI; reported plan to retire Dragon for ISS trips.
- Publicly at odds or allied with: rivals Blue Origin and OpenAI; NASA is its biggest customer.
- Handle with care: post-IPO stock swings; FAA and Memphis permit disputes; no invented valuations.
