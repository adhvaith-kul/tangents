# @coinbase: background (as of 14 November 2026)
- What it is and who leads it now: the largest US crypto exchange, Nasdaq-listed (COIN); CEO Brian Armstrong, President and COO Emilie Choi.
- Recent:
  - 1 Oct: International Exchange derivatives clients moved to Deribit; options, spot margin and unified portfolios rolling out; Coinbase Pro returns by year end.
  - 19 Oct: stablecoin partnership with Thunes; 28 Oct: RLUSD listed.
  - 29 Oct: Q3 net income $719M on about $1.2B revenue; USDC held on platform a record $22B; prediction markets over $200M annualised revenue.
  - 30 Oct: deadline for EEA users to move non-MiCA stablecoins, converted to USDC after.
- Products and priorities: the "everything exchange" (crypto, stocks, prediction markets, derivatives), Coinbase One, Base, USDC, AI-agent payments.
- Publicly at odds or allied with: Binance is the chief global rival; banks oppose its stablecoin rewards; the SEC dropped its case in 2025; funds the Fairshake PAC.
- Handle with care: outages, hacks and customer-data breaches; May 2026 layoffs; no invented listings or earnings figures.
