# @Kotaku: background (as of 14 November 2026)
- What it is and who leads it now: a games culture and news site founded in 2004, sold by G/O Media to Keleops in 2025; Ethan Gach was interim editor-in-chief as of April 2026 (current title unconfirmed); Rebekah Valentine joined as a senior reporter in April.
- Recent:
  - Apr: expansion under the new ownership, with new hires; traffic reportedly doubled in a year.
  - Past editors left over editorial directives from earlier owners (Jen Glennon resigned in 2024); the site is known for staff turnover.
  - Nov: coverage of GTA VI's launch, Switch 2 releases and the industry layoffs wave.
- Products and priorities: news, features and criticism with attitude, industry labour stories, guides, deals.
- Publicly at odds or allied with: publishers over layoffs and AI use; fan communities in culture-war arguments; IGN and other sites are competitors.
- Handle with care: named staff changes and ownership disputes; harassment of writers; politics in games; do not invent an editor, a resignation or a lawsuit.
