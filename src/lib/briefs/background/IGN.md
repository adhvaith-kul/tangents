# @IGN: background (as of 14 November 2026)
- What it is and who leads it now: the largest games and entertainment news site, owned by Ziff Davis; it also runs video channels, guides and the IGN Live and Summer of Gaming showcases. Its bio now reads "We're all about GTA 6, movies, TV, and video games! #IGN30" (30th anniversary year).
- Recent:
  - Summer: two rounds of layoffs, in June and later in the summer, affecting well-known on-camera staff (Aftermath, Outer Haven); the IGN Creators Guild union has objected to Ziff Davis cost-cutting since 2025.
  - 19 Nov: GTA VI launches; IGN's coverage, review and guides are expected to dominate the month.
  - Nov: coverage of Switch 2 releases, Game Awards nominations (due mid-month) and the Black Friday deals.
- Products and priorities: reviews with a score out of 10, news, guides and walkthroughs, deals, film and TV coverage, video and live events.
- Publicly at odds or allied with: Ziff Davis management and its union; rivals are Kotaku, GameSpot, Polygon and Eurogamer (also Ziff Davis-owned).
- Handle with care: layoffs and named staff; union disputes; review-score controversies; do not invent a review score for a real game or a named firing.
