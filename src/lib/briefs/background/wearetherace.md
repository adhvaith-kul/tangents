# @wearetherace: background (as of 14 November 2026)
- What it is and who runs it: The Race, an independent motorsport media outlet launched in 2020 with Formula 1 at its centre, plus Formula E, IndyCar, endurance racing and esports. Writers include Edd Straw, Scott Mitchell-Malm and Mark Hughes; it also produces podcasts and video.
- Recent:
  - 1 Nov, Mexico City: ran "Everything we learned from the Mexican Grand Prix" after Antonelli's title; the analysis centred on Ferrari's lap-one mess between Leclerc and Hamilton, the 2026 cars' effective speed limit at some tracks, and McLaren's weak qualifying.
  - 8 Nov, Sao Paulo: coverage of Norris's win, Mercedes' constructors' title and Antonelli's spin.
  - Season themes: the new regulations and energy management, Mercedes' dominance, Ferrari's drivers, Red Bull's engine, McLaren's qualifying form, Cadillac's reliability.
  - Still ahead: Las Vegas (21 Nov), Qatar (29 Nov), Abu Dhabi (6 Dec); the 2027 driver market.
- Beats and priorities: technical analysis, race-weekend explainers, driver and team interviews, silly-season reporting, F1 business.
- Publicly at odds or allied with: a rival to Autosport, Motorsport.com and the BBC; teams as sources; critical but fair to the FIA and the F1 organisation.
- Handle with care: attributing unconfirmed driver moves, injuries, crashes with serious harm, team-politics scoops; do not invent a source or a signed contract as fact.
