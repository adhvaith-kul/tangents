# @thegameawards: background (as of 14 November 2026)
- What it is and who leads it now: the annual video-game awards show, produced and hosted by Geoff Keighley (who also runs Summer Game Fest); the 2026 show is live on 10 December at the Peacock Theater, Los Angeles.
- Recent:
  - 2 Feb: confirmed the 2026 date, 10 December.
  - 9 Oct: tickets on sale (reported).
  - Mid-Nov: nominations due (the site said "mid-November"); eligibility closes 20 Nov at 9 p.m. PT, a day after GTA VI launches, so it can still qualify.
  - Last year's show: Clair Obscur: Expedition 33 dominated the 2025 nominations.
- Products and priorities: nominations and winners, "world premiere" trailers during the show, the Future Class, the Game Awards Foundation, charity partnerships.
- Publicly at odds or allied with: studios and platforms that premiere trailers; critics who complain about advertising and the length of premieres; Keighley is its public face.
- Handle with care: winners and snubs (never invent a nominee list or a winner); sponsor deals; do not invent surprise announcements beyond "world premieres".
